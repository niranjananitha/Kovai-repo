# TaskFlow User Guide

TaskFlow is a simple personal to-do list. Only you can see your tasks.

## Important notes and warnings

- **The first load can take up to a minute.** The app runs on a free server that goes to sleep when nobody uses it. If you see "Waking up the server", just wait.
- **Open the link in Chrome or Safari.** Browsers built into apps like Instagram, WhatsApp or LinkedIn may block Google sign-in. Use "Open in browser" if you see a blocked or blank sign-in. (Email and password sign-in is not affected.)
- **Cookies must be enabled** in your browser, otherwise you cannot stay signed in.
- **Deleting a task is permanent.** After you press delete you have 5 seconds to press **Undo**. After that it is gone for good. If you close the page within those 5 seconds, the task is kept.
- **There is no "forgot password".** If you forget your password, you cannot recover that account, so choose one you will remember or use Google.
- **Sign out on shared computers** when you are finished.
- **You are signed out after 7 days** and need to sign in again.

## How to open the app

Open the web address you were given (for example `https://your-app.onrender.com`) in Chrome or Safari, on a phone or a computer.

## Create an account or sign in

You can use an email and password, or your Google account.

**Create an account**
1. Choose the **Create account** tab.
2. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters. The hint under the box tells you when it is long enough, and the eye button shows or hides what you typed.
3. Press **Create account**. You are signed in straight away.

**Sign in**
1. Choose the **Sign in** tab, enter your email and password, and press **Sign in**.
2. Or press **Continue with Google** and choose your account.

**Good to know**
- If you try to create an account with an email that already has one, the page offers **Sign in instead** and keeps your email typed.
- After 5 wrong passwords for the same email in 15 minutes, you are asked to wait a few minutes.
- If you first made an account with a password and later use **Continue with Google** with the same email, the two are joined. The old password stops working and you sign in with Google from then on.

## Your tasks

After signing in you see your tasks on the **Board**: three columns, **Planned**, **In Progress** and **Complete**. Your name is shown at the bottom of the left menu, beside **Sign out**. On a phone, open the menu with the button at the top left.

### Add a task
1. Press **New task**, or press **N** on your keyboard.
2. Type a **Title** (required, up to 120 characters; a counter appears near the limit). Add a **Description** if you like (up to 1000 characters).
3. Press **Enter** to save. In the description box, **Enter** also saves and **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. **Esc** closes the window without saving.

New tasks always start as **Planned** and appear at the top of that column.

### Move a task to another status
- **Drag** the task by its grip (the dots on the left of the card) to another column, or
- use the **status menu** on the task and choose Planned, In Progress or Complete. This is the way to do it on a touch screen or with the keyboard.
- With the keyboard: Tab to the grip, press **Space** to pick the task up, use the **arrow keys** to move it, and press **Space** again to drop it.

Any status can change to any other. When a task becomes **Complete**, its title gets struck through and the progress ring in the menu fills a little more.

### Switch between Board and List
Use the **Board / List** buttons at the top (or in the menu on a phone). The List shows the same tasks as compact rows grouped by status. The app remembers your choice.

### Filter and search
- The menu lists **All, Planned, In Progress, Complete** with the number of tasks in each. Pick one to show only that status.
- Type in the search box (or press **/**) to find tasks by words in the title or description. Press **Esc** in the box to clear it.

### Delete a task
Press the bin button on the task. The task disappears and a message appears at the bottom with **Undo**. You have 5 seconds to undo it; after that it is deleted permanently.

### Sign out
Press the sign-out button at the bottom of the left menu.

## Known limitations

- You cannot edit a task's title or description after creating it. Delete it and add a new one.
- No due dates, priorities, tags or sharing.
- No password reset, email confirmation, account deletion or data export.
- Designed for up to a few hundred tasks.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The page shows a spinner for a long time | The server is waking up. Wait up to a minute. |
| "Could not reach the server" | Check your internet connection, then try again or refresh the page. |
| "Email or password is incorrect" | Check both and try again. If you signed up with Google, use **Continue with Google**. |
| "Too many attempts" | Wait a few minutes, then try again. |
| "An account with this email already exists" | Press **Sign in instead**. |
| Google sign-in pop-up is blank, blocked or missing | Open the link in Chrome or Safari instead of an app's built-in browser. Allow pop-ups for the site. Or use email and password. |
| "Sign-in was cancelled or failed" | Press the Google button and try again. |
| "Your session has expired" | Sign in again. Sessions last 7 days. |
| You keep getting sent back to the sign-in screen | Make sure cookies are enabled and you are not in a private window that blocks them. |
| A length or "please enter" message | Fix the field it points to: title 1 to 120 characters, description up to 1000, password 8 or more. |
| A task move or delete did not work | A red message at the bottom explains why, and the task goes back where it was. Try again. |
| I deleted something by mistake | Press **Undo** within 5 seconds. After that it cannot be recovered. |
