# TaskFlow User Guide

TaskFlow is a simple personal to-do list. Only you can see your tasks.

## Important notes and warnings

- **The first load can take up to a minute.** The app runs on a free server that goes to sleep when nobody uses it. If you see "Waking up the server", just wait.
- **Open the link in Chrome or Safari.** Browsers built into apps like Instagram, WhatsApp or LinkedIn may block Google sign-in. Use "Open in browser" if you see a blocked or blank sign-in.
- **Cookies must be enabled** in your browser, otherwise you cannot stay signed in.
- **Deleting a task is permanent.** It cannot be undone.
- **Sign out on shared computers** when you are finished.
- **You are signed out after 7 days** and need to sign in again.

## How to open the app

Open the web address you were given (for example `https://your-app.onrender.com`) in Chrome or Safari, on a phone or a computer.

## Sign in

1. On the first screen, press **Sign in with Google**.
2. Choose your Google account in the pop-up.
3. You arrive at your task list. Your name appears at the top right, next to **Sign out**.

Google is the only way to sign in. There is no separate password.

## Add a task

1. Type a **Title** (required, up to 120 characters). When you get close to the limit, a counter appears.
2. Optionally type a **Description** (up to 1000 characters).
3. Press **Add task** (or press Enter in the title box). The button is greyed out until the title is filled in.

New tasks always start as **Planned** and appear at the top of the list.

## See and filter your tasks

Your tasks are listed newest first. The buttons **All, Planned, In Progress, Complete** filter the list, and the number in brackets shows how many tasks are in each group.

## Change a task's status

On a task, use the **Status** drop-down and choose Planned, In Progress or Complete. You can move between any two statuses, in any direction.

## Delete a task

1. Press **Delete** on the task.
2. The task asks "Delete permanently?". Press **Yes, delete** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep it.

## Sign out

Press **Sign out** at the top right.

## Known limitations

- You cannot edit a task's title or description after creating it. Delete it and add a new one.
- No due dates, priorities, tags, search or sharing.
- No way to delete your account or export your tasks.
- Designed for up to a few hundred tasks.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The page shows a spinner for a long time | The server is waking up. Wait up to a minute. |
| "Could not reach the server" | Check your internet connection, then try again or refresh the page. |
| Google sign-in pop-up is blank, blocked or missing | Open the link in Chrome or Safari instead of an app's built-in browser. Allow pop-ups for the site. |
| "Sign-in was cancelled or failed" | Press the sign-in button and try again. |
| "Your session has expired" | Sign in again. Sessions last 7 days. |
| You keep getting sent back to the sign-in screen | Make sure cookies are enabled and you are not in a private window that blocks them. |
| "Please enter a title" or a length message | Fill in the title (1 to 120 characters) and keep the description under 1000. |
| A task change did not save | A red message explains why. Fix the problem and try again. |
